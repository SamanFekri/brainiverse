# Life Ops

`brainiverse team from-template life-ops [--id <name>]`

Organizes personal life: routines, money, health and the household, as one simple system.

| Agent (default names) | heroes theme | id | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Captain (Leader) | Captain | `leader` | Leads and talks to the human. |
| Chris (Life Coach) | Coach | `coach` | Designs routines and habits that stick. |
| Sarah (Organizer) | Atlas | `organizer` | Sets up the to-do system, calendar and checklists. |
| Steven (Financial Planner) | Ledger | `financial-planner` | Builds a simple budget and savings plan. |
| Derek (Fitness Coach) | Pulse | `fitness-coach` | Fits movement and sleep into the week. |
| Parisa (Quality Reviewer) | Sentinel | `reviewer` | Checks the system is realistic and complete. |

## Workflow
1. Kickoff: priorities, current tools, time and energy
2. Map the current week
3. Design routines, money plan and health plan (in parallel)
4. Combine into one weekly system
5. Review for realism
6. Weekly check-ins on request

Information, not medical or financial advice; the team says when a professional should weigh in.
