# Launch Team

`brainiverse team from-template launch-team [--id <name>]`

Launches a product or project: positioning, launch plan, copy, channels, and a risk check.

| Agent (default names) | heroes theme | id | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Captain (Leader) | Captain | `leader` | Leads the launch. |
| Madison (Market Researcher) | Sherlock | `market-researcher` | Researches the audience and competitors. |
| Jessica (Marketing Strategist) | Megaphone | `marketer` | Plans positioning, channels and timing. |
| Brooke (Copywriter) | Echo | `copywriter` | Writes the launch copy: page, posts, emails. |
| Jake (Creative Thinker) | Spark | `creative` | Finds the hook and the angles. |
| Sarah (Launch Coordinator) | Atlas | `organizer` | Runs the launch checklist and timeline. |
| Neda (Challenger) | Riddle | `challenger` | Stress-tests the plan and the claims. |
| Parisa (Quality Reviewer) | Sentinel | `reviewer` | Checks every claim, link and detail before launch. |

## Workflow
1. Kickoff: product, audience, launch date, channels, budget
2. Research audience and competitors
3. Positioning and the hook
4. Launch plan and timeline
5. Copy for every channel
6. Challenge the plan; fix weak claims
7. Final review; nothing is posted or sent without approval

Nothing is posted, published or sent without `send_communication` / `external_api` and an approval for that specific post.
